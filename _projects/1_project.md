For my bachelor's thesis at RWTH Aachen University's Chair for Machine Learning and Inference, I worked on action classification through dynamic scene graph generation from image sequences.

The project explored how relational structure can be recovered from visual sequences and used for downstream action understanding. The thesis was graded 1.0 and is available through the chair's thesis archive.
