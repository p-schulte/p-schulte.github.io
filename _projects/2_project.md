VISA is research on learning lifted symbolic planning domains from image sequences. The work connects visual sequence understanding with discrete relational structure and symbolic planning.

The project appears as an oral presentation at the GenPlan Workshop at ICAPS 2026, with equal contribution by Ulzhalgas Rakhman and Paul Schulte.
