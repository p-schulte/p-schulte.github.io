During my RISE Worldwide research internship at Carnegie Mellon University's Robotics Institute, I developed an MPC framework with Control Barrier Functions for safety-critical trajectory planning.

The implementation targeted real-time planning in JAX on CPU-based F1TENTH hardware, reducing reliance on GPU-heavy approaches. The work contributed to the USDOT/Safety21 final report <i>Risk-Aware Warning and Control for Interactive Traffic Safety</i>.
